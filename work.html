COMMISSIONS

I make 3D artworks, and you can commission custom pieces if you want! Scope is discussed before any work begins.

  • Full digital illustration — character, scene, starship, or concept
  • Custom UI / HUD design, Custom Star Trek LCARS UI, etc.
  • Starship designs, concepts, panoramas
  • Multiple artstyles such as sketch/manga/stylized/cartoonish/realistic...

Slots are limited. Please check the footer status indicator before reaching out. Prices vary by complexity — please check KO-FI for further details!

I open commission ONLY via KO-FI. Exceptions might be made in some cases.

  • Make sure that your Commission slot is booked and confirmed before payment.
  • Scope of the Comm is discussed before booking the Commission.
  • NO inappropriate content. I will NOT entertain any NSFW Commissions.
  • All commissions are posted on my socials, if you want to commission anonymously, please specify!.
  • For private commission (aka no posting anywhere at all)- please specify beforehand. This may increase cost of the artwork.
  • A small watermark at the corner of the artwork is applied in all cases. For personal use, the artwork without said watermark will be available (for added cost). However, for sharing, watermarked version must be used!
  • I reserve the right to deny a commission. If it is inappropriate/not doable at the moment, I will politely decline it.
  • Exact pricing will be updated once I open Commissions!

Slots are limited. Please check the footer status indicator before reaching out. Prices vary by complexity — please check KO-FI for further details!

REQUESTS

Somewhat like a community suggestion queue. Free requests are open pretty much the whole time.

  • Feel free to reach out on my socials if you have a specific request! That's the process of a request.
  • There is currently no queue in order, requests are handled via rule of cool. Yes.
  • The final product may or may not be faithful to a request, but usually I try to keep it close to the original as possible
  • NO inappropriate content. I will NOT entertain any NSFW Requests.
  • All requests are posted on my socials, if you want to commission anonymously, please specify!.
  • There are no "private" requests. Anonymous requests can be made. Some rare cases might be exceptions, please reach out for those.
  • It takes time to make artworks, especially since requests are free. Completion is not guaranteed!

Requests are not commissions. They influence the work but don't guarantee execution. Follow socials to catch when they go live.
